# 🍕 Crust & Fire - Interactive Gourmet Dining Menu

An ultra-responsive, zero-lag **Interactive Restaurant Menu Website** featuring high-definition culinary photography, instant horizontal scroll browsing, and live price recalculation matching your menu cards.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Zero-Lag & Buttery Smooth**:
   - Heavy 3D WebGL models replaced with high-definition food photography.
   - 60+ FPS performance on every phone, tablet, and PC.
   - Instant loading with crisp, mouthwatering imagery.

2. **Horizontal Scroll (Scroll Right ➔)**:
   - In each category (starting with Pizzas), scroll right or click the **Next/Prev (◀ / ▶)** arrows to browse every flavor.
   - The main stage smoothly transitions images.
   - Thumbnail cards show real photo previews, item names, and starting prices.

3. **Vertical Category Flow (Scroll Down ▼)**:
   - **🍕 Artisan Pizzas**: All 13 flavors with Small, Medium, Large, XL size selectors and extra toppings toggle.
   - **🍔 Gourmet Burgers**: All 13 chicken and beef smash burgers with "+Rs. 250 Meal Upgrade (Fries & Drink)".
   - **🌯 Wraps & Shawarma**: Authentic Arabic shawarmas, crispy rolls, and wraps.
   - **🍗 Injected Broast & Starters**: Half/Full Broast meals, cheesy fries, hot wings, and mozzarella sticks.
   - **🥩 Sizzling Steaks & Pasta**: Sizzling fillets, fettuccine alfredo, and Turkish platters.
   - **🥤 Chilled Drinks & Shakes**: Lotus Biscoff, chocolate, strawberry, and mint margaritas.
   - **🎁 Value Combos & Deals**: Curated feast boxes for couples and families.

4. **Dynamic Price Recalculation**:
   - Real-time price updating on portion sizes (Small: Rs. 700 / Med: Rs. 1350 / Large: Rs. 1750 / XL: Rs. 2490).
   - Instant total updates with item quantities and upgrades.

5. **Slide-Out Cart & Direct WhatsApp Ordering**:
   - Slide-out order drawer with subtotal, items list, and quantity adjusters.
   - Automatically compiles a clean, itemized receipt and opens WhatsApp to place the order with your kitchen!

---

## 🚀 How to Run

- **Option 1**: Double-click `run-server.bat` in this folder. It launches the local server and automatically opens `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.
- **Option 2**: In terminal, run `python -m http.server 8000` and open [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000).
